# Exploratory descriptor analysis

This follow-up is specified after inspection of the fresh-seed forecasting results. It is exploratory, even though its implementation and choices are committed before this analysis runs. The eight data realizations are held out for regression prediction but do not constitute a newly untouched test study.

The primary question is whether a validation-only temporal descriptor mismatch adds predictive value beyond model identity and validation forecast distortion. The target is common forecast-probe MSE averaged equally across the five shifted conditions and their four sequences. There is one row per data realization and model, including untrained controls and the uncompressed persistence reference: 112 rows clustered in eight realizations. Model identity absorbs the fixed architecture, initialization and training-budget differences for each named procedure. This predicts performance on a new realization of the same generator using the same named models, rather than on unseen architectures.

Selected checkpoints and linear probes are restored from the existing hash-locked archive without fitting. Input and latent descriptors are computed on the 127-sample input prefix of each of the two validation sequences. Each descriptor is averaged over features. The primary extra predictor is the mean of the two sequence-level squared distances, with lag-one correlation differences divided by two. Descriptors measured on shifted test sequences are excluded from prediction features. Validation MSE is taken from the locked forecast probe and independently reproduced from its saved coefficients.

Baseline predictors are one indicator per model and validation forecast-probe MSE. The primary extended predictor adds the scalar validation descriptor distance. Secondary extensions add the three squared component mismatches or the three latent descriptors instead. All are reported regardless of direction. In-distribution common-probe MSE and native forecast MSE are secondary endpoints. The native analysis excludes PCA and random projection because their native objective is reconstruction, and uses native validation forecast MSE as its distortion predictor.

Each outer fold withholds every row of one data realization. On the remaining seven realizations, ridge regularization is chosen by another leave-one-realization-out loop from [0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 1, 10]. Features are centered and scaled and the target is centered using the fitting rows of each inner or outer fold only. The intercept is unpenalized. The objective is mean squared residual plus the penalty times squared coefficient norm. The first penalty wins ties. Each candidate's inner loss is the mean of its seven held-out realization losses. No outcome transformation, feature search or clipping is used.

The primary regression loss is squared prediction error on forecast MSE, averaged over models within a held-out realization and then equally over the eight realizations. The paired difference is baseline loss minus extended loss, so positive values favor the extra predictor. Report every fold, their mean and the number with positive differences. Repeated rows, sequence conditions and model cases are not treated as independent replicates. No significance threshold, confidence interval, causal interpretation or necessary-complexity-preservation claim is attached to eight exploratory folds. Model-identity-only and validation-distortion-only predictors provide secondary reference losses.

Existing compensated orthogonal rotations are summarized separately. They preserve common-probe predictions while potentially changing coordinatewise descriptors. Positive scaling and time shuffling are diagnostic controls. These summaries describe coordinate dependence rather than prospective generalization predictions. Quantum descriptors observe retained single-qubit Z readouts, not the complete density matrix. Finite-sample LZ76 and permutation entropy are not MDL codelengths.

Verification requires the original archive SHA-256, unchanged selection-lock hashes, all 112 restored validation-probe errors matching locked metadata to 1e-10, complete finite features and predictions, explicit disjoint inner/outer realization memberships and unchanged source evidence. Tests check all checkpoint families and ensure changing an outer held-out target cannot affect its fitted predictions or penalty choice.
